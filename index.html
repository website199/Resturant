Our Story

Crafted with fire,
served with soul

At Ember & Oak, we believe that exceptional dining begins with respect for the land. Our kitchen celebrates the rich bounty of Canadian terroir — from the pristine waters of the Pacific to the fertile soils of Ontario's farmland. Every dish is a dialogue between fire and ingredient, tradition and innovation.

Executive Chef Marcus Riel brings two decades of experience from Lyon and Copenhagen, infusing classical technique with Nordic simplicity. The result is a menu that honours place, season, and the quiet artistry of the kitchen.
